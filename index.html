How it works

Two ways in

Traveling somewhere?

  1. Search your city. See who knows it and what they're into.
  2. Pick how you want help. A list to read, questions answered, or a walk together. All free.
  3. Land like you live there. Skip the tourist traps and go where locals go.

Know a city well?

  1. Apply as a local. Tell us your city and what you know.
  2. Pick how you help. Share your list, answer messages, or meet up.
  3. Become the go-to for your city. Travelers already ask you where to go. Now they can find you.
